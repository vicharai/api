About

Vichar is a single API for models from many providers. It exists for developers who don't want to manage a separate account, key and billing relationship for every lab they use.

One key and one OpenAI-compatible endpoint reach the whole catalogue. When a provider slows down or fails, requests reroute to another healthy one, so your application keeps answering.

What we care about:

  • Transparent per-token billing at the listed model rate.
  • Reliability through automatic failover across providers.
  • Privacy by default: we store request metadata (tokens, cost, latency, provider), not your prompts or responses.

Simple, usage-based pricing

Pay for the tokens you use at the listed model rate. No subscription, no seat fees, no monthly minimum.

  • Listed per-token rates

    Every request is billed at the model's published input and output rates. See the models page for current prices.

  • 5% platform fee on top-ups

    A flat fee applies when you add credits. A $20 top-up costs $21.

  • $20 minimum top-up

    Prepaid credits start at $20. Auto top-up is available but optional.

  • Invoices for every top-up

    Each purchase produces an invoice in your dashboard and in your inbox.

  • Spend limits per key

    Cap spend on any API key so a runaway workload cannot drain your balance.

  • Failed requests are not billed

    Requests that fail before a provider answers do not deduct credits.

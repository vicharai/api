Vichar is prepaid: you top up credits and every request deducts its metered token cost.

- **Minimum top-up**: $20. A flat 5% platform fee applies to the top-up amount (a $20 top-up costs $21 and adds $20 in credits).
- **No subscription, no monthly minimum**: credits are spend-based only.
- **Auto top-up**: optional. When your balance falls below a threshold you set, the saved card is charged automatically.

- **Invoices**: every top-up produces an invoice you can download from the
dashboard under **Billing**. - **Failed requests are not billed**: if a provider
fails before completing the request, nothing is deducted.

## Refunds

Unused top-ups can be self-refunded from the dashboard when all of these hold: the payment completed, it is the most recent top-up, it is within 14 days of the charge, you are the organization owner, and less than 20% of the purchased credits have been used. Eligibility is evaluated automatically when you open the refund dialog; ineligible payments show the reason.

## Spend limits and caps

Beyond per-key limits, organizations have daily and monthly spend caps that grow with account age and lifetime spend (see [Rate limits](/rate-limits)).

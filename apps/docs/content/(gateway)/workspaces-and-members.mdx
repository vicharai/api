An organization is your workspace: it owns the credit balance, API keys, projects, and the activity log.

<ThemedImage
	alt="Members page"
	basePath="/screenshots/members"

- **Roles**: owners manage billing, members, and settings; admins manage keys, projects, and members (not billing); project admins manage a single project; developers use keys within a project. Members can be invited by email from **Members** in the dashboard.
- **Shared credits**: all keys across all projects draw from the organization's prepaid balance. Per-member budget constraints can cap how much a member's keys may spend.
- **Projects** group keys and settings inside the workspace.

Create additional organizations or switch between them from the workspace picker in the top-left of the dashboard.

Limits apply per organization across all its keys.

## Defaults for new accounts

- **Requests**: 600 requests/minute on inference endpoints.
- **Concurrency**: 100 in-flight inference requests.
- **Spend caps**: $25/day and $250/month model spend; $250/day in top-ups.

## Tiers

Limits multiply with account age and lifetime spend:

| Tier | Qualifies when                            | RPM    | In-flight | Daily spend | Monthly spend | Top-ups/day |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------- | ------ | --------- | ----------- | ------------- | ----------- |
| 0    | new account                               | 600    | 100       | $25         | $250          | $250        |
| 1    | 7 days old or $10 spend (min age 1 day)   | 1,200  | 200       | $100        | $1,000        | $500        |
| 2    | 30 days or $100 spend (min age 3 days)    | 2,400  | 400       | $500        | $5,000        | $2,500      |
| 3    | 60 days or $1,000 spend (min age 7 days)  | 6,000  | 1,000     | $5,000      | $50,000       | $10,000     |
| 4    | 90 days or $5,000 spend (min age 14 days) | 12,000 | 2,000     | $15,000     | $200,000      | $20,000     |

## When limited

Over-limit requests return `429` with a JSON error. Responses include `Retry-After` and `RateLimit-Reset` (seconds until the window resets) plus `RateLimit-Remaining: 0`. Back off for the indicated interval and retry.
